## How I work

1. **Find the signal.** Read the market, audience, product and tension worth naming.
2. **Frame the narrative.** Make the complex clear, credible and locally relevant.
3. **Stage the moment.** Choose the launch, leader, room or story that gives the idea energy.
4. **Prove the movement.** Connect attention to follow-up, adoption, trust, pipeline and learning.
